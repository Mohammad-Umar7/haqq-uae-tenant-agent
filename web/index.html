§1

Three numbers most tenants never see.

Dubai's tenancy rules are short and exact. Landlords know them. Tenants usually meet them for the first time in a WhatsApp message.

90days

Notice of a rent increase

A landlord who wants to raise the rent, or change any term, must tell you at least 90 days before the contract expires.

Law No. 26 of 2007, Art. 14

0–20% cap

How much it may rise

The cap depends on how far your rent sits below the RERA Rental Index average for similar homes.

% below the RERA average → maximum increase

Decree No. 43 of 2013, Art. 1

12months

Notice to evict

To end your tenancy for the owner's own use, a sale or a renovation, the landlord must give 12 months' notice by notary public or registered mail. A WhatsApp message does not count.

Law No. 26 of 2007, Art. 25(2)

§2

One orchestrator. Six specialists. One case file.

An agent only needs AI when the job needs language or judgement. Anything that can be counted is counted by code, so a model never does the maths.

  1. 01

    Orchestrator

    AI Claude, tool-use loop

    Talks to you, decides which specialist to ask next, and writes the answer. Every other agent works for it.

    claude-haiku-5-5 · adaptive thinking

  2. 02

    Contract Analyst

    AICode reads once, scans by rule

    Reads the contract once into structured data, then scans every clause for red flags such as a “non-refundable” deposit.

    read_contract_once · clause_risk_scan

  3. 03

    Calculators

    Code plain Python

    Works out the rent cap under Decree No. 43 of 2013 and the notice deadline under Law No. 26 of 2007. Arithmetic, not guesswork.

    rera_rent_increase_check · notice_deadline_check

  4. 04

    Law Researcher

    Code BM25 search

    Finds the official article behind every point, and checks that each citation in the answer really came back from its search.

    search_tenancy_law · citation audit

  5. 05

    Drafter

    CodeAI template, then Claude

    Writes the first draft from a template, then revises it once after the Critic's review, in English, Arabic or both.

    draft_letter · revise_letter

  6. 06

    Critic

    AICode the landlord's lawyer

    Attacks the draft's numbers, dates, citations and tone before your landlord can. A code check verifies every figure first.

    critic_review · automatic check

  7. 07

    Notice Reader

    AI Claude vision

    Reads a screenshot of the landlord's message: the new rent, the date it was sent and how it was delivered. Text aimed at an AI is reported, never obeyed.

    read_notice_image

            5. 
  
          

  
          
    
          Where it falls short
          
    
          The keyword red-flag scan found 12 of 15 planted red flags (80% recall) and raised one false one (92.3% precision). Reworded clauses slip past keyword rules. In full AI mode Claude also reads every clause, but how much of the gap that closes has not been measured yet. The law base covers the core tenancy law and the rent decree, not every regulation, and it is only as current as its last update.
